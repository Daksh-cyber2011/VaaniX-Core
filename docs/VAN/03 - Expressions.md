# VAN Design Bible

## 🎭 Chapter 3 — Expressions

Version: 1.0
Status: Approved
Last Updated: 2026-07-17

1. Philosophy

Van communicates emotions primarily through:

Eyes
Beak
Wings
Body posture
Feather tuft
Small movements

Every expression should be readable within 0.5 seconds, even without text.

2. Emotion Levels

Every emotion has three intensities:

🟢 Light

🟡 Medium

🔴 Strong

Example:

Happy →
😊 Smile

😁 Big Smile

🥳 Celebration

This makes animations feel natural instead of repetitive.

11. Emotion Rules

Van should NEVER appear:

❌ Evil

❌ Creepy

❌ Aggressive

❌ Mocking

❌ Sarcastic

❌ Bored with the user

❌ Emotionless

❌ Arrogant

Even when correcting mistakes, Van should encourage instead of shame.

12. Accessibility

Expressions must remain understandable:

Without text.
For children and adults.
At small icon sizes.
Across different cultures.
In both light and dark mode.
