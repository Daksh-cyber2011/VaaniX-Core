# VAN Design Bible

## 👕 Chapter 5 — Outfits

Version: 1.0
Status: Approved
Last Updated: 2026-07-17

1. Philosophy

Van's appearance should evolve with the user's journey.

Outfits are not just cosmetic.

They should:

Celebrate achievements.
Reflect seasons.
Match user preferences.
Make the companion feel alive.
Encourage long-term engagement.

Outfits must never affect learning performance or create pay-to-win mechanics.

Every user should always have a beautiful default Van.

2. Default Outfit

This is the identity of Van.

It should always be available.

Clothing
Blue hoodie
White drawstrings
Small VaaniX logo on chest
Sleeves slightly oversized
No pants
No shoes
Orange feet visible

This outfit becomes the visual identity of the brand.

6. Premium Rule

V1 has:

NO paid cosmetics.

Everything is earned.

Later (optional):

Special collaboration outfits

Limited edition cosmetics

Community contest winners

But the default Van should never feel inferior.

Strict Coach

These are small variations, not entirely different characters.

10. Brand Consistency

No outfit should ever hide:

Feather tuft
Eye shape
Beak
Hoodie silhouette (unless intentionally replaced by another category)

Van must always remain recognizable.

11. Color Rules

Outfits should complement Van's yellow feathers.

Avoid:

❌ Neon colors

❌ Overly dark palettes

❌ Horror themes

❌ Military camouflage

❌ Violent symbolism

Use:

Soft blues

Teals

Greens

Warm reds

Orange accents

Cream

White

Navy

Purple

12. Outfit Animation Rules

Clothes should move naturally.

Scarf swings.

Cape flows.

Backpack bounces slightly.

Hat tilts.

Loose hoodie moves subtly.

Everything should feel lightweight.

13. Accessibility

Accessories should never:

Hide facial expressions.

Cover the eyes.

Block the beak.

Make gestures unreadable.

Learning always comes first.

14. Future Expansion

The wardrobe system should support:

500+ outfits

16. Golden Rules

Van's wardrobe must always:

Reward learning, not spending.
Reflect progress.
Stay clean and modern.
Never distract from studying.
Keep Van instantly recognizable.
Feel joyful and meaningful.
