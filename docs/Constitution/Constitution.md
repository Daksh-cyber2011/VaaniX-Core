The Constitution of VaaniX
Founding Constitution


1. Purpose
VaaniX exists to become a trusted learning companion. It is built to make learning enjoyable, meaningful and lifelong rather than limited to exams. The goal is to help learners build confidence, communication skills and the art of learning.

This chapter should evolve as VaaniX grows. Every future feature, product, language, pricing model and AI capability should remain aligned with these principles. The constitution is a living document that guides long-term decisions rather than a static specification.
