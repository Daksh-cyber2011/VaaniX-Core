# VAN Design Bible

## Chapter 2 — Visual Design

Version: 1.0
Status: Approved
Last Updated: 2026-07-17

1. Design Philosophy Van is not just a mascot. Van is the student's AI companion who makes learning feel alive, personal, and enjoyable. The design should immediately communicate warmth, intelligence, curiosity, and trust. The design must: Be instantly recognizable. Look friendly at every age. Feel premium yet playful. Never appear childish or overly cartoonish. Scale well from a tiny app icon to full-screen illustrations. Remain visually consistent across the app, website, social media, merchandise, and future animations.

2. Character Style Art Style: Modern flat illustration. Soft 2D vector design. Inspired by Duolingo's simplicity but with a unique identity. Clean geometric shapes. Rounded forms. No sharp edges. Premium startup aesthetic. Animation-friendly. Avoid: Hyper realism Anime proportions Pixar realism Heavy gradients Excessive textures Thick outlines.

3. Overall Silhouette Van should be recognizable even as a black silhouette. Silhouette Rules: Large rounded head. Small rounded body. Short wings. Small tail. Short legs. Wide beak. Feather tuft on top. The silhouette should instantly say: "That's Van".

4. Body Proportions Head: ≈45% of total height Body: ≈35% Legs: ≈10% Feet: ≈10% These proportions maximize cuteness while keeping animation easy.

5. Head Design The head is almost circular. Not perfectly round. Slightly wider horizontally. No sharp corners. Soft transitions everywhere.

6. Eyes Eyes are Van's strongest emotional feature. Shape: Large rounded oval. Placement: Wide apart. Style: Simple black pupils. Tiny white highlight. No visible sclera during neutral expressions. The eyes should communicate emotion more than the mouth.

7. Beak Short. Rounded. Soft edges. Never pointy. The beak should stretch naturally during speech and expressions.

8. Feather Tuft Van has a signature feather tuft. It should: Slightly bounce during movement. Help express emotion. Never disappear. Stay simple.

9. Wings Short rounded wings. They behave like arms. Van points, waves, claps, celebrates, shrugs, and gestures using wings.

10. Feet Small orange feet. Rounded. Never overly detailed. Simple enough for smooth animation.

11. Tail Small rounded tail. Visible mainly from side and back angles. Used subtly during movement.

12. Color Palette Primary Feather: Warm soft yellow. Secondary Feather: Light cream. Beak: Soft orange. Feet: Orange. Eyes: Dark charcoal. Accent Color: VaaniX Blue. Error State Accent: Soft Red. Success Accent: Fresh Green. Celebration Accent: Golden Yellow. No neon colors. No overly saturated colors.

13. Clothing Default outfit: Blue hoodie. Small VaaniX logo. No pants. No shoes. No gloves. Simple zipper. Occasionally sleeves can be rolled.

14. Accessories Accessories should never replace Van's identity. Allowed: Backpack Glasses Graduation cap Headphones Santa hat Umbrella Notebook Pencil Magnifying glass Coffee mug Scarf Crown Explorer hat Accessories must remain removable.

15. Facial Rules Van's face should never become: Angry in a scary way. Creepy. Overly realistic. Emotionless. Mean. Even frustration should feel encouraging.

16. Lighting Flat lighting. Soft shadows only. No dramatic cinematic lighting. No harsh reflections.

17. Brand Personality Every illustration should communicate at least one of these: Curious Helpful Intelligent Friendly Encouraging Calm Cheerful.

18. Scalability Van must work perfectly: As an app icon. As a notification icon. As a chat avatar. As a sticker. As an onboarding illustration. As a loading animation. As a website mascot. As social media artwork. As merchandise.

19. Design Consistency Rules The following should NEVER change: Head proportions Eye style Beak shape Feather tuft Feather colors Hoodie design Body proportions Overall silhouette These define Van's identity.

20. Future Compatibility Van should be designed so it can later become: Fully animated. Lip-synced. Interactive. Live 2D. 3D. AR-compatible. VR-compatible. Robot-compatible. Plush toy. Collectible figurine. Brand ambassador.
